This page talks to your Raspberry Pi signer over USB. It never sees your recovery words or PIN. No transaction is sent to the blockchain until you press broadcast.

Honest prototype. Satooshi Pro is a hobby build, not an audited commercial wallet. Use test funds. Your seed is generated and kept on the device and never leaves it. This page is separate from your Satooshi browser wallet and does not read, migrate or overwrite it.
